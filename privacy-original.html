Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Thank you for playing Tower of Infinity. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our game.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the game, we may collect the following information:

  • Anonymous player identifier.
  • Game progress.
  • Achievements.
  • Inventory and unlocked items.
  • Leaderboard scores.
  • Purchase history for digital products.
  • Device identifiers required for advertising and purchases.
  • Basic diagnostics and crash information.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide gameplay functionality.
  • Save your progress.
  • Restore purchases.
  • Deliver purchased content.
  • Display leaderboards.
  • Improve game stability.
  • Serve advertisements where applicable.

Advertising

Tower of Infinity uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google may collect and process certain device identifiers and advertising data according to its own Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Randomized Rewards

The game contains optional randomized cosmetic rewards (gacha/loot box mechanics). Items obtained through these systems have no real-world monetary value.

Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services:

  • Microsoft PlayFab
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Google Play Billing / Unity IAP

Please refer to each provider's privacy policy for more information.

Children

Tower of Infinity is not directed toward children under the minimum age required by applicable laws without parental consent.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure communication protocols where appropriate to help protect your information.

Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct or request deletion of certain data.
