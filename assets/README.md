JPEG derivatives use quality 90 at full original resolution, without cropping or redrawing. Original PNG files remain unchanged. These seven JPEGs total about 6 MB, versus about 30 MB for their originals. Only configured images are copied into deployment output. The mascot keeps its authentic background and PNG format.

No standalone logo was supplied; the authentic logo appears within the hero poster and the header uses a text wordmark. Set assets.logo in site.config.json when a standalone original is supplied. Additional skin icons remain available as source assets but are not presented as unverified gameplay features.

To replace an image, update the filename and width/height pair in site.config.json, then rebuild. Portrait art is displayed completely. Gallery images lazy-load. Captions and alt text are bilingual; embedded English text requires official Spanish image variants. Future WebP/AVIF optimization may further reduce image transfer sizes.
