# Required developer privacy review

Evidence is limited to the original `index.html` (July 2026), `app-ads.txt` and the supplied brief. No game source, manifests, SDK settings, backend code or store questionnaires are available. The original policy is preserved byte-for-byte in privacy-original.html. The root now serves the homepage per the corrected requirement. All disclosures are retained in the new English policy and translated into Spanish. Presentation date does not change policy effective date.

PlayFab, AdMob, UMP and Google Play Billing / Unity IAP are explicitly named in the inherited policy. This is evidence of published claims, not independent verification. StoreKit is not asserted active; iOS implementation must be inspected first.

Before publishing an amended policy or submitting iOS, verify:

- SDK/data inventory on each platform: identifiers, IP addresses, advertising, diagnostics, analytics, authentication, purchases and indirect provider collection. The absolute no-email/no-location declaration and anonymous-ID terminology need review.
- PlayFab authentication, saved fields, leaderboard visibility, linking, deletion and local/cloud progress. Confirm inherited purchase restoration claims, particularly for consumable diamonds.
- AdMob formats and personalization, UMP consent/withdrawal by region, and iOS ATT where applicable. Listing UMP does not prove a working consent flow.
- Google Play/Unity IAP verification, receipts/tokens and backend processing. Confirm Apple StoreKit integration before adding iOS processing disclosures.
- Actual retention criteria for progress, accounts, logs, transactions, support mail and backups; deletion exceptions. No periods were invented.
- Purposes, legal bases by region, controller identity/details, user requests, consent withdrawal and complaint channels.
- Provider processing regions, international transfers and safeguards. No locations or safeguards were invented.
- Age audience, store age rating, parental consent and child-directed ads. The inherited minimum-age wording is vague.
- Actual encryption/security measures supporting inherited claims.
- Support-email processing: visitors necessarily share email when writing to support. The new policy explains this separately without removing the original game-data declaration.
- Cosmetic-only randomized reward wording: supplied Lucky Drop art also shows diamonds and a gameplay-looking boost. Check actual rewards before amending the inherited claim.
- Google Play Data Safety and Apple App Privacy consistency; reward odds and paid-draw store requirements.
- Qualified review of equivalent EN/ES revisions and an approved new effective date for substantive amendments.

Official refund sources used by support: [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637) and [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223). No eligibility, deadlines, refunds or recovery are guaranteed.
