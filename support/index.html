Questions, purchase issues, or a block that just won’t behave? Get in touch with KSR Origin.

Include your device, operating system, game version, and a description of what happened. If the game displays a player ID, include it. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, or payment card details.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap to place the moving block on your tower. Aim for precise alignment, keep stacking, and try to beat your high score.

How do perfect landings work?

A precise placement can earn a perfect landing. Chain them together for combo streaks. Follow the feedback shown in the game; exact timing thresholds are not published here.

How can I unlock skins?

Check the skins and reward options in the game. Randomized reward draws can include cosmetic items. Read the available rewards and any displayed odds or costs before spending diamonds; a draw does not guarantee a particular item.

How do I earn diamonds?

Diamonds are available through gameplay and optional in-app purchases. Check the game for the current earning opportunities and prices.

How do daily rewards work?

Open the daily rewards section and follow the instructions shown in the game. Availability, reset times, and claim conditions are determined by the current game version.

How do leaderboards work?

Leaderboards compare player scores. Check the in-game ranking screen for the available categories and periods. If your score is missing, check your connection and contact support with your player ID if available.

What if my purchase is missing?

Check the purchase status in the store account used to buy it, confirm your internet connection, and restart the game. Do not buy the same item again to troubleshoot. If it still has not arrived, email support with the product, purchase date, platform, and order reference, redacting unrelated personal information. Purchases are processed by the corresponding platform. Restoration and refunds are not guaranteed.

What if a rewarded ad gives no reward?

Check your connection and allow the ad to finish normally. If no reward appears, note the time, reward, device, and game version, then contact support. Avoid repeatedly watching or purchasing to resolve the issue. We cannot promise compensation.

Can lost progress be recovered?

Contact support before reinstalling or clearing app data; those actions may remove local information. Describe what changed and include your player ID if available. Recovery depends on the data that remains available and cannot be guaranteed. Do not send account credentials.

How do I report a bug?

Email support with the steps to reproduce the issue, expected and actual behaviour, your device, OS, and game version. Screenshots or a short recording can help; hide personal information first.

Purchase and refund help

Use the official process for the platform where you made the purchase. The platform decides eligibility.
